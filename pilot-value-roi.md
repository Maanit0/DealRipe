# DealRipe Pilot: Value + ROI, in the reps' own words

Source: check-in calls with Juan Lopez (Jul 24) and Eduardo Bencomo (Jul 21).
Both are Mark's most thorough reps and the pilot users. Everything below is
backed by direct quotes from those calls.

---

## The headline

Both reps came from doing this by hand. Juan wrote notes on paper and retyped
them into Rolldog. Eduardo pasted transcripts into ChatGPT/Claude and copied the
output into Salesforce and Rolldog. DealRipe removes that entire step, captures
the qualification detail that matters, keeps the CRM current for Mark, and does
it across English and Spanish calls. Neither rep had anything they wanted removed.

The deeper win is not the minutes saved, it's what the rep does with the attention
that's freed up: with note-taking off his plate, Juan is fully present on the call,
running higher-quality discovery and missing fewer qualification points. Better
calls close more deals. That is the value that a time-saved number understates.

Juan, on the "would you miss it" test:
> "I would definitely notice it... it's like going back to my notes that were
> handwritten, typing that back into Rolldog... I'm gonna have none of the
> traction... so yeah, I would miss it."

Juan, overall:
> "It's an amazing tool, and coming from using nothing, it's all pluses."

---

## 1. It kills post-call admin (the quantified ROI)

This is the clearest, rep-confirmed number.

**Before:** Juan handwrote notes during the call, then transcribed and typed them
into Rolldog, including his next step.
> "From before, I would actually manually write down my notes and then I would
> transcribe... that would take me maybe a good two minutes or less per deal...
> and in that time I put in my next step in there too."

**Now:**
> "So now it's seconds, because now I just copy the things that I could copy and
> paste it into Rolldog."

**Eduardo's old workflow was even heavier:**
> "The way I'm doing it now is I need to [take] the transcript... and then get a
> recap [from Claude]... so this actually helps me."

**ROI math (conservative):**
- ~2 min saved per call on note transcription + next-step entry, dropping to seconds.
- Juan runs 3-4 discovery calls a day.
- That's roughly 6-8 min/day/rep on note transcription alone, before counting
  Rolldog field entry and recap generation.
- Rolldog write-back removes a second manual step (see #4).
- Eduardo does the same double entry into both Salesforce and Rolldog.

Framed for Mark: DealRipe converts admin minutes on every deal into selling time,
and it compounds across every rep and every call, every day.

---

## 2. It captures everything that matters, accurately

Juan on what the recap covers:
> "It recaps what I discussed, their timeline, what their pain points are, what's
> missing, what my next steps are, and what I should validate before moving forward."

Juan on the Core Logistics recap (a real deal):
> DealRipe caught budget was set, that Brian reads the proposal and makes the final
> decision, the timeline (they want to start next month instead of end of year),
> that they're switching off CargoWise, and that they're actively comparing vendors
> on price and functionality. Juan: "That was like amazing."

This is the trust story for Mark: the qualification fields in the CRM reflect what
was actually said on the call, not what a rep remembered to type later.

---

## 3. It makes the rep more present, which makes the calls better (the real prize)

Time saved is the feature. A more present rep is the outcome that actually moves
revenue, and it's the more important half of the story.

When Juan isn't splitting attention between the conversation and scribbling notes,
he's fully in the call: listening, reading the room, going deeper on pain, and not
letting qualification points slip. Higher-quality discovery leads to better-qualified
deals, cleaner next steps, and more closes. This is the compounding value that a
per-call time number understates.

Juan:
> "I could just sit back a little bit more and know that the note-taker is taking
> my notes for me... it just [makes me] more engaging on the call."

He also named the failure mode DealRipe removes, even for a veteran:
> "We get very excited as sales reps when we hear all the buying signals, and [we]
> just forget to cover some of the points."

The logic for Mark: reps who aren't managing their own note-taking run better
discovery, miss fewer qualification gaps, and close more. The admin time saved is
real, but the lift in call quality is where the pipeline impact comes from.

---

## 4. It writes back to the CRM so pipeline data stays trustworthy

DealRipe writes recap detail and qualification sub-fields (competitor, budget,
drivers, and the People tab for economic buyers) back into Rolldog automatically
once a deal has an opportunity ID.

Concrete example: on Core Logistics, Brian was not in Rolldog. DealRipe caught
that he was the budget owner and decision maker from the transcript and is set to
write him into the People tab. Eduardo confirmed this is exactly the kind of manual
CRM upkeep he wants off his plate.

For Mark specifically: this is the difference between a pipeline he has to trust the
reps to maintain and one that maintains itself from the calls.

---

## 5. Pre-call briefings save prep and cover for BDR gaps

Juan preps 10-15 min before each discovery call. The briefing helps most when the
BDR hasn't passed context along, or when he's remote and can't get to Salesforce.
> "That is very helpful, because if for no reason the BDR hasn't passed that info
> along, then I'm already reading that and getting the insights, so that's beautiful."

> "Salesforce mobile struggles to find those notes, so the email comes in handy."

Eduardo on the briefing as process:
> "This is good, it's good guidance... it's worth it to have this kind of process
> set up [for] new salespeople."

## 6. No-show detection and follow-up drafts

DealRipe flags no-shows automatically. It caught Juan's no-show with Ace Customs
and Eduardo's with Duty Free Americas. The no-show follow-up draft feature came
straight from Eduardo's suggestion and is already shipped.

Juan, on seeing the no-show catch: he thought it was "so cool."

## 7. Manager visibility (the Mark angle)

Juan, unprompted, made the management case himself:
> "From a management perspective, like Rich, my VP... they aren't on the call, they
> can easily just read that and it feels like it's my deal, because I'm saying the
> names... even if you're not involved, you'd think you were in the deal. So for a
> management perspective, that's a good brief and recap for them as well."

This is the weekly digest / pipeline-read value proposition, validated by a rep
without being led to it.

## 8. It works in Spanish (a Magaya-specific differentiator)

Juan flagged that DealRipe correctly captured a discovery call conducted entirely
in Spanish:
> "I took a call in Spanish, and they got the recap right... they got everything,
> that was awesome... they were both in Spanish."

Given how much of Magaya's pipeline is Latin America, clean Spanish-language
capture is core to their book of business, not a nice-to-have.

---

## 9. It scales the top reps' judgment to new hires

Both reps are 10+ year veterans ("my colleagues call me the machine" - Juan). Their
instinct is that the real leverage is onboarding newer reps on the best reps' habits.

Juan:
> "Definitely, especially for a new hire that doesn't know the process, doesn't know
> what the next steps are."

Even the machine forgets things:
> "We get very excited as sales reps when we hear all the buying signals, and [we]
> just forget to cover some of the points... that might happen to me too."

DealRipe learns the questions Juan and Eduardo ask to fill gaps, then surfaces those
same questions to newer reps. The pilot with the two best reps is tuning the system
to Magaya's best practices.

## What's still open (be honest with Mark)

- **Deliverability:** recaps and briefings were landing in the reps' junk folders
  (Barracuda/Outlook), so some of the value was invisible until we fix the allowlist.
  This inflates the upside once fixed. Confirmed with Juan on the Jul 24 call.
- **Accuracy at scale:** reps love the recaps but haven't rigorously validated field
  accuracy across many deals yet. Worth a light audit before broad rollout.
- **Coverage:** most discovery deals live in Salesforce, not Rolldog, so write-back
  only fires once a deal is a Rolldog opportunity. Salesforce integration expands
  where the value lands. Both reps confirmed Salesforce is their system of record.
