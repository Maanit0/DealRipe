**Read the 29 carefully before repeating it.** The script matches on time and
recipient rather than on the draft id, so a rep who sent from another address, or

at creation, which makes this exact going forward rather than retroactively.

half the 29 are real, that is the single most actionable rep finding in the
pilot, and it is exactly what a draft feature exists to fix.
