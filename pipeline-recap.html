What this call was about
This was a net-new pipeline review call led by Mark Buman, who also introduced Rebecca Jasch as the new SVP of Marketing to the net-new sales team. The team discussed the current pipeline status (~$300K with ~$2M+ still to close in roughly two months), seasonal slowness due to summer vacations, and the importance of deal hygiene and accurate pipeline data for weekly board updates.

• Mark introduced Deal Ripe, an AI tool being tested to help generate weekly deal summaries and track deal movements; Eduardo has been using it and Mark is expanding the test.
• Rebecca Jasch was formally introduced to the net-new sales team as the new SVP of Marketing; a larger in-person meeting with Rebecca, Mark, Eduardo, Mitch, Gus, and Jeanette is planned in the coming weeks.
